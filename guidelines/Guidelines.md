# Saketh Farm Mango E-commerce App

## Design System

### Color Palette
The app uses a warm, tropical mango-inspired color scheme with yellow and reddish-orange gradients.

### Typography
- **Display Font**: Quicksand - rounded and friendly for headings
- **Body Font**: DM Sans - clean and readable for body text

### Features
- Product catalog with add-to-cart functionality
- Shopping cart with quantity management
- WhatsApp integration for orders
- Responsive navigation
- Organic farm storytelling

### Pages
1. Home - Product listing with mango banner
2. About - Farm story and organic practices
3. Contact - WhatsApp community links
4. Cart - Order summary with WhatsApp checkout
